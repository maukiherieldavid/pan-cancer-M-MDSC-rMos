# Analyses performed outside R

## SPP1 expression and survival in LUAD

The bulk-tissue SPP1 tumour-versus-normal comparison was generated using the GEPIA2 
expression-analysis module. Statistical significance follows the platform-defined comparison.

Overall-survival analyses for SPP1 were evaluated using TCGA LUAD RNA-seq and 
clinical data accessed through GEPIA2 and OncoDB 2.0. Patients were stratified 
into high- and low-expression groups using the median SPP1 expression level 
as the cutoff. Survival distributions were compared by log-rank testing and 
hazard ratios were obtained from univariate Cox proportional-hazards analysis as 
provided by the respective platforms.

## Multiplex immunohistochemistry

The mIHC panels are microscopy-derived experimental data. Image acquisition and 
qualitative marker assessment are described in the manuscript Methods and are not 
generated by the R workflow in this repository.
